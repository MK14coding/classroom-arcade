Time Racer

Choose your car, read the clock, earn Time Tokens and build your garage.

How to Play

Look carefully at the analogue clock and choose the correct digital time.

🕐 Level 1 starts with o'clock times.
🕧 Level 2 introduces half-past times.
🕒 Level 3 introduces quarter-past times.
⏱️ Correct answers earn Time Tokens for the garage.

Garage Upgrades

⭐ Stickers
🛞 Rims
🏁 Wings
🔥 Boosts
⚙️ Engines
🏆 Champion style
